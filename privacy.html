Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how Channel Publisher ("the tool") handles data. The tool is used only by its operator to post the operator's own videos to the operator's own TikTok accounts.

Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic account information (open ID, display name, avatar) to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok after the operator signs in.
  • Posting settings returned by TikTok (available privacy levels, maximum video length) and the status of each post.

How we use it

Only to upload and publish videos the operator has approved, and to check that the right account is used. We do not read, collect or analyze other users' content, followers or messages.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer in a local file excluded from version control. They are never shared, sold, or sent to third parties, and are not written to logs or chat messages.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any data. Videos are sent only to TikTok, at the operator's request.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the operator disconnects the tool or the tokens expire. Revoking access in TikTok settings (Security > Manage app permissions) or deleting the local token file removes the connection immediately.

Children

The tool is not directed to children and is used only by its adult operator.

Changes

We will update this page if the way data is handled changes.
