Channel Publisher

Channel Publisher is a private, single-operator tool used to publish short videos that we produce ourselves to our own TikTok accounts (InfoBro and HutSound).

It is not offered to the public. Only the operator signs in, and only with TikTok accounts that the operator owns.

How it works

  1. The operator finishes a short video and reviews it.
  2. The operator approves publishing in a private Telegram chat.
  3. The tool uploads the approved video file to the operator's own TikTok account using the TikTok Content Posting API, with the privacy level and interaction settings the operator chose.
